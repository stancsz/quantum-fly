# Quantum Fly

## What it is

Quantum Fly is a reproducible, bounded research prototype for testing connectome-inspired numerical decision structures, classical and optional quantum simulation, causal historical evaluation, and evidence-bearing local agent communication.

## Who uses it

Researchers and engineers who need to reproduce bounded experiments and inspect negative as well as positive evidence. It is not a brokerage, trading service, clinical system, or general-purpose autonomous executor.

## Job to be done

Run a clearly bounded experiment from documented data and settings, preserve its receipt, compare against simpler controls, and communicate what the evidence does and does not establish.

## Current delivery surface

Local Python commands, JSON receipts, a persistent local Fly loop, an allowlisted read-only Janelia research adapter, and a bounded LeanRouter smoke integration. There is no deployed public service or production URL.
