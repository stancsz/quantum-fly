# Code of conduct

Be respectful, specific, and evidence-minded. Discuss the work, not the person. Welcome careful questions, reproductions, and negative results. Do not pressure contributors to share private data, strategies, credentials, or resources beyond the declared experiment.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, and abusive or deceptive behavior are not accepted. Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove contributions that violate these expectations and may restrict participation when needed to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately through the maintainer's GitHub profile. Avoid including sensitive personal information in a public issue.
