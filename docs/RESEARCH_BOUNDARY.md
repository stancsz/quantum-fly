# Research boundary

## Verified present capability

Quantum Fly is a reproducible research prototype. In a prepared local environment, a reader can rerun the bounded MaleCNS scan and subgraph experiment, causal historical evaluation, structure comparisons, optional PennyLane measurements, local persistent communication loop, allowlisted read-only research request, and request-correlated LeanRouter smoke call. The associated commands and receipts are the evidence for these bounded capabilities.

## Not established by this repository

The prototype is not established as operationally reliable software. The current evidence does not establish long-term stability, model quality or cost advantage, universal tool execution, whole-brain materialization, quantum advantage, profitability, investment efficacy, or production readiness. A successful smoke call is one runtime sample, not an uptime or quality guarantee.

Research outputs, backtests, and model responses are not investment advice. No live brokerage, account, order, public-posting, or autonomous execution capability is provided. Any stronger claim requires a separate Steward-defined goal with claim-specific tests, runtime evidence, and explicit limits.
