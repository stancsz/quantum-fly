# Quantum Fly North Star

Quantum Fly should become an independently reproducible, locally auditable research baseline for testing whether connectome-inspired structure adds robust value to causal portfolio experiments. It must remain easy to falsify, bounded in compute and authority, and honest about what each receipt proves.

Success is not a profitable backtest, a quantum result, a biologically evocative diagram, or a passing local test suite. Success is an independent user being able to reconstruct the declared inputs, run the registered controls, inspect failures and uncertainty, and reach the same bounded assessment without relying on undocumented machine history.

## Product principles

- Evidence before narrative. Code, tests, input hashes, runtime receipts, and explicit limitations support every completed claim.
- Controls before complexity. A connectome or quantum path must compete with transparent classical and no-graph baselines under matched causal evaluation.
- Negative results remain first-class. A failed gate narrows the claim and is never tuned away after observation.
- Bounded autonomy. No brokerage, accounts, orders, credentials, arbitrary command execution, public posting, or unattended external mutation.
- Reproducibility before scale. Clean setup, exact data identity, deterministic configuration, and portable verification matter more than more nodes, agents, or qubits.
- Separate capability layers. Local research, public reproducibility, unattended runtime, investment claims, and production use receive separate GO or NO-GO decisions.

## Current completion rubric

The active contract is the 11 acceptance criteria in `GOAL.md`. North Star completion is measured mechanically as the number of criteria with code, focused tests, and recorded runtime evidence, divided by 11. A criterion is not complete because work is planned or documentation says it is.

The requested 90% threshold means at least 10 of 11 criteria are independently verified. The Builder may update implementation and evidence records, but may not weaken the denominator or acceptance wording.

The Steward authorized an MIT software license and creation of the first accepted Git baseline on 2026-09-12. Data attribution remains separate, and a commit is evidence of traceability rather than proof of publication or deployment.

## Definition of the research baseline

At the 90% threshold, an independent reviewer should be able to verify all but at most one of these capability areas:

1. turnover-consistent transaction costs and causal reward semantics;
2. request-bound market-cache provenance and offline corruption tests;
3. exact MaleCNS manifest and bounded preparation or import;
4. clean-environment reproduction with a machine-readable receipt;
5. traceable Git and isolated verifier baseline;
6. pinned runtime and optional-quantum environment verification;
7. preregistered classical controls, multiple periods or assets, seeds, and uncertainty;
8. bounded concurrent runtime behavior and visible timeout limits;
9. strict loader, numeric, persistence, and JSON input rejection;
10. minimum shareable-project metadata, including an explicit software-license decision;
11. a fresh criterion-to-evidence audit with separate capability-layer decisions.

The stronger claims remain out of scope unless a later goal defines new evidence: profitable or suitable investment decisions, quantum advantage, biological fidelity, full-brain execution, reliable provider service, live trading, and production readiness.

## Ownership

`NORTHSTAR.md` owns durable direction and the completion measure. `GOAL.md` owns the active acceptance contract and execution evidence. Research protocols own preregistered thresholds. Runtime receipts report observed results and must not silently broaden this North Star.
